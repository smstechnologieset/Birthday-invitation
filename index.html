His First Year

A Year of Firsts

This year has been a collection of beautiful firsts — the first sleepy smile, the first contagious giggle, the first tiny hand wrapped around a finger, and the first wobbly steps across the living-room floor.

Every day with Matania has been a gift of pure joy. Now the first of many birthdays is here, and we can't imagine celebrating it anywhere but surrounded by the people we love most.

"May your days be full of sunshine, your laughter never-ending, and your little heart always know just how deeply you are loved."

Happy First Birthday, Matania
